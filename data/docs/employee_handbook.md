# Helios Robotics Employee Handbook

Version 7.1 — effective January 1, 2026. Owner: People Operations. This handbook applies to all
full-time and part-time employees of Helios Robotics, Inc. and its subsidiaries. Where local law grants
more generous terms, local law prevails.

## 1. Working at Helios

Helios Robotics builds autonomous mobile robots and collaborative arms for warehouses and factories.
We have offices in Austin (headquarters), Berlin, Singapore and Sao Paulo. Our values are
*safety first*, *ship with care*, and *own the outcome*.

### 1.1 Working hours

The standard work week is 40 hours. Core collaboration hours are **10:00 to 15:00 local time**, Monday to
Friday; outside core hours employees may arrange their schedule with their manager. Non-exempt employees
must record all hours worked in the HR system by Friday 17:00 local time each week.

## 2. Paid Time Off (PTO)

### 2.1 Accrual

Full-time employees accrue PTO at a rate of **1.67 days per month** (20 days per year), starting on their
first day of employment. After **5 years of continuous service**, the accrual rate increases to
**2.08 days per month** (25 days per year). Part-time employees accrue PTO pro rata to their scheduled hours.
PTO accrues during paid leave but not during unpaid leave.

### 2.2 Carry-over

Employees may carry over a maximum of **5 unused PTO days** into the next calendar year. Carried-over days
must be used by **March 31** of the following year; any carried-over days not used by that date are
forfeited. PTO above the carry-over limit at December 31 is forfeited unless local law requires payout.
PTO is not paid out on termination except where required by law.

### 2.3 Requesting time off

Requests of up to 2 days need 1 week's notice; longer requests need 3 weeks' notice. Managers should
respond within 3 business days. Teams in the on-call rotation must arrange cover before PTO is approved.

### 2.4 Sick leave and holidays

Employees receive **10 paid sick days per year**, which do not carry over. A doctor's note is required
only for absences longer than 3 consecutive working days. Helios observes 11 company holidays per year;
the regional holiday calendar is published each December.

### 2.5 Parental leave

Primary caregivers receive **16 weeks** of fully paid parental leave and secondary caregivers receive
**6 weeks**, available within 12 months of a birth, adoption or foster placement.

## 3. Remote and Hybrid Work

### 3.1 Hybrid schedule

Helios operates a hybrid model. Employees may work remotely up to **3 days per week**; the remaining days
are spent in the assigned office. Teams agree on shared anchor days, published in the team charter.
Roles that operate lab equipment or the robot test floor may require additional on-site days.

### 3.2 Fully remote arrangements

Fully remote work requires written approval from a **Vice President** and People Operations, is reviewed
every 12 months, and is only available in countries where Helios has a legal entity. Working from another
country for more than 20 working days in a calendar year requires prior approval because of tax and
immigration implications.

### 3.3 Home office

Hybrid and remote employees receive a one-time home office stipend of **$750** (or local equivalent) for
ergonomic furniture and peripherals, plus a monthly internet allowance of $50. Company laptops must be used
for all work; personal devices may only access email and chat through the managed mobile app.

## 4. Expenses and Reimbursement

### 4.1 Submitting expenses

Business expenses must be submitted in the Ledger expense tool **within 30 days** of the date the expense
was incurred. Expenses submitted after 30 days require director approval and expenses older than 90 days
are not reimbursed. Itemised receipts are required for every expense over **$25**.

### 4.2 Approval thresholds

Expenses up to $500 are approved by the employee's manager. Expenses over **$500** additionally require
approval from the department head, and any single expense over $5,000 requires Finance approval before it
is incurred.

### 4.3 Meals and entertainment

Client meals are reimbursed up to **$85 per person**, including tax and tip, and the attendees and business
purpose must be recorded. Team meals require director pre-approval and are capped at $50 per person.
Alcohol is reimbursable only at client meals and in moderation.

### 4.4 Non-reimbursable items

Fines, personal entertainment, first-class upgrades, and memberships not approved in advance are not
reimbursable. Travel-specific rules (flights, hotels and per diem) are in the Travel Policy.

## 5. Learning and Development

Each employee has an annual learning budget of **$1,500** for courses, conferences and certifications.
Unused budget does not roll over. Conference attendance outside the budget needs director approval.

## 6. Conduct and Safety

All employees working on the robot test floor must complete safety training before access is granted and
renew it every 12 months. Report any safety concern to the Safety Officer immediately; Helios has a strict
no-retaliation policy for good-faith reports.
