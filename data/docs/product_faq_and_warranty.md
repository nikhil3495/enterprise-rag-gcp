# Helios Product FAQ and Warranty Guide

This guide answers common customer questions about the Helios product line and describes warranty and
support coverage. Last updated: October 2026.

## 1. Product Line Overview

Helios sells four products: the **Atlas AMR** autonomous mobile robot, the **Orion Arm** collaborative
robot arm, the **Vega Vision Kit** perception module, and the **Nova Controller** edge compute unit.
All products are managed through Helios Cloud and run Helios OS.

## 2. Atlas AMR

### 2.1 Specifications

* Maximum payload: **150 kg**
* Top speed: **1.8 m/s** (since Helios OS 4.2)
* Battery: LiFePO4, **10 hours** of typical runtime per charge
* Charging: fast charge to 80% in **90 minutes** on the Atlas Dock
* Ingress protection: IP54; operating temperature 0 °C to 45 °C
* Navigation: 360° lidar plus stereo cameras; no floor markers required

### 2.2 Frequently asked questions

**Can Atlas operate in cold storage?** No. Atlas is rated down to 0 °C; a cold-storage variant is on the
roadmap. **How many robots can a site run?** Helios Cloud supports up to 500 Atlas units per site.
**Does Atlas need Wi-Fi?** Atlas keeps operating for up to 30 minutes without connectivity and
re-synchronises when the connection returns.

## 3. Orion Arm

* Degrees of freedom: 6-axis
* Payload: **12 kg**
* Reach: **1,300 mm**
* Repeatability: ±0.03 mm
* Force-torque sensing in the wrist (enabled by Helios OS 4.3)

Orion is certified for collaborative operation under ISO 10218 and can work next to people without
fencing when a risk assessment allows it.

## 4. Vega Vision Kit and Nova Controller

The **Vega Vision Kit** is a 12 MP stereo camera module with on-board depth estimation, used for picking
and inspection. The **Nova Controller** is a fanless edge computer that coordinates up to 8 Orion Arms
and Vega kits on a work cell.

## 5. Warranty

### 5.1 Standard warranty periods

| Product | Standard warranty |
| --- | --- |
| Atlas AMR | 24 months |
| Orion Arm | 18 months |
| Vega Vision Kit | 12 months |
| Nova Controller | 36 months |

The **Atlas AMR battery** is covered separately for **36 months or 2,000 charge cycles**, whichever comes
first. Warranty starts on the date of delivery. It covers defects in materials and workmanship and
excludes damage from misuse, unauthorised modification, or operation outside rated conditions.

### 5.2 Extended warranty

**Helios Care+** extends the standard warranty by **24 months** for any product. It must be purchased
within **90 days** of delivery and includes advance replacement of failed parts.

### 5.3 Returns and RMA

Products that are dead on arrival are replaced free of charge if reported within **30 days** of delivery.
For warranty claims, open a support ticket with the robot serial number; Helios issues an RMA number and a
prepaid shipping label within 2 business days.

## 6. Support Tiers

| Tier | Coverage | First response target |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Standard | Email and portal, business hours (Mon–Fri) | 1 business day |
| Professional | Phone and portal, 12x5 | 4 hours |
| Premier | **24/7 phone** and portal, dedicated Technical Account Manager | 1 hour for SEV1 |

Standard support is included with every product. Professional and Premier are annual subscriptions
priced per site. Premier customers also receive quarterly business reviews and early access to beta
releases.
