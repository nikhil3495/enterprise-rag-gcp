Helios Robotics Travel Policy

Policy ID: FIN-014. Effective April 1, 2026. Owner: Finance. This policy covers business travel for all employees. General expense rules (submission deadline, receipts, approvals) are in the Employee Handbook.

1. Booking

All flights, hotels and rental cars must be booked through the corporate travel portal so that traveller location is known for duty-of-care purposes. Book flights at least 14 days in advance whenever possible; bookings made less than 7 days before departure require a reason code.

2. Air Travel

2.1 Class of service

  • Flights with a scheduled flight time under 6 hours: economy class.
  • Flights of 6 to 10 hours: premium economy is allowed.
  • Flights over 10 hours of scheduled flight time: business class is allowed.
  • Business class on shorter flights requires written approval from a Vice President.
  • First class is never reimbursed.

2.2 Baggage and changes

One checked bag is reimbursed per trip; additional bags are reimbursed when carrying demo or test equipment. Change fees are reimbursed when the change is driven by business needs.

3. Lodging

Hotel nightly rates are capped by city tier (see section 4). Stays longer than 14 nights in one city should use serviced apartments booked through the travel portal.

4. Per Diem by City Tier

Meals and incidentals are reimbursed as a daily per diem; receipts are not required for per diem. On the first and last day of a trip, 75% of the per diem applies.

5. Ground Transport

Use public transport, taxis or ride-share where practical. Rental cars must be compact or intermediate class; decline the rental company's insurance because Helios is self-insured. Use of a personal car is reimbursed at $0.70 per mile.

6. Safety and Approvals

International travel requires manager approval at least 21 days before departure and registration with the travel-security provider. Travel to countries rated high-risk requires approval from the Chief Operating Officer.
